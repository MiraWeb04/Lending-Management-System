## The form does not match a 20-statement instrument

Form C.2 has 26 statements:

- Items 1–6, administrator only
- Items 7–12, collector only
- Items 13–18, borrower only
- Items 19–26, all roles

The planned headcount written on the form is 1 administrator + 1 collector + 30 borrowers = 32. Role-only items are supposed to use N = 1, N = 1, or N = 30. Shared items are supposed to use N = 32 unless someone left an item blank.

The tally sheet C.3 has one blank row for each of the 26 statements. The frequency columns are empty. The criterion summary cells for the weighted means are empty. The tallied-by and checked-by lines are blank.

Chapter 5 of the same updated file says the numeric results of the tests remain to be entered from the recorded sheets, and that the conclusions do not include a measured acceptability score. Table 4.6 in Chapter 4 says unit testing passed 6 of 6 and integration testing passed 7 of 7. Those statements disagree with Chapter 5.

The five means supplied in the review request average to 4.78. That arithmetic check only shows that the overall figure matches the average of those five reported means. It does not show that any respondent gave those scores.

## What was not done

- Individual answers were not inferred from either mean.
- No synthetic respondent grid was written.
- Neither 4.81 nor 4.48 was accepted as a verified research result.

## What the researcher must provide

1. The original completed forms, or a faithful tally, for all 26 statements.
2. A count of who answered each item, separated by administrator, collector, and borrower.
3. The worksheet that turns those frequencies into the means that will stay in Table 4.7.
4. A decision about which manuscript figures are current: the 4.81 series named for this review, or the 4.48 series printed in the file on disk.
5. The signed acceptance page, if Chapter 4 continues to say the client accepted the system.

Until that packet is present, Table 4.7 and the overall mean should be described as reported figures that this review could not recompute.
