How to use this file
This packet supports Chapters I to V. It has three parts. Part A is the reference list for the sources that can be matched to a published work, plus the author–year mentions in Chapter II that still need the copy the group actually read. Part B is the pre-development questionnaire, interview guide, and observation checklist. Part C is the evaluation form, tally sheet, test-record sheets, and acceptance form.
The forms are blank. Chapter IV already reports weighted means and test results. Those figures should be copied onto the tally and test sheets only from the accomplished forms. This file does not invent individual answers.
Respondents named in Chapter III: one lending administrator, one collector, and thirty borrowers of the Poblacion, Sominot office.

Frequency (f) is the number of respondents who chose a scale value. N is the number who rated that item. The weight (w) is the scale value 5, 4, 3, 2, or 1.
Weighted mean  =  (5f5 + 4f4 + 3f3 + 2f2 + 1f1)  ÷  N
Percentage  =  (f ÷ N) × 100
Planned N for the office evaluation: 1 administrator + 1 collector + 30 borrowers = 32. An item that only one role answers uses that role’s N (1, 1, or 30), not 32.
