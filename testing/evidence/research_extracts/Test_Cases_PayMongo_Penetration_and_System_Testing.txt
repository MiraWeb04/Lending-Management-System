PM-01
Sign in as the collector assigned to an unpaid loan. Choose Bank Transfer or E-Wallet, enter an amount greater than zero and within the amount due, and create a Dynamic QR Ph payment.
A pending PayMongo QR Ph transaction is stored, and the QR payment page opens for that loan.
After the collector submitted a valid Bank Transfer or E-Wallet amount, a pending QR Ph transaction was created and the Dynamic QR Ph page opened. The page showed the payment amount, the selected method, and the PayMongo Payment Intent.
Pass
PM-02
Open a newly created QR Ph payment and check the loan balance before the borrower pays.
The QR code is shown, and the loan balance stays unchanged until PayMongo confirms the payment.
The QR image was displayed with the amount, method, and expiry. The outstanding balance and the loan status stayed the same while the transaction remained pending.
Pass
PM-03
Choose Cash as the payment method on an assigned unpaid loan.
Dynamic QR Ph is not offered for Cash.
The Create Dynamic QR Ph action stayed hidden when Cash was selected. A cash collection could still be recorded through the regular payment form.
Pass
PM-04
Start a QR Ph payment with a blank, zero, or non-numeric amount.
The QR payment is not created, and a validation message is shown.
The amount was rejected. No PayMongo transaction was stored, and the loan balance was not changed. The page asked for a valid payment amount greater than zero.
Pass
PM-05
Start a QR Ph payment with an amount greater than the current total amount due.
The QR payment is not created, and the amount due is not exceeded.
The payment was rejected. The message stated that the payment cannot exceed the current total amount due, and no QR transaction was stored.
Pass
PM-06
Sign in as a collector who is not assigned to the selected loan and try to create a QR Ph payment.
The QR payment is refused because the collector is not assigned to that loan.
The request was refused with the message that the collector is not assigned to the loan. No QR code was created and no payment was recorded.
Pass
PM-07
Try to start a PayMongo QR Ph payment while signed in as a borrower, or without a collector session.
Only an assigned collector can start a PayMongo QR Ph payment.
The borrower session and a signed-out visit could not start a QR Ph payment. The system kept the action limited to a collector.
Pass
PM-08
Try to create a second QR Ph payment while an earlier QR Ph attempt for the same loan is still unresolved.
The second attempt is blocked until the open attempt is finished, refreshed, or marked expired.
The system kept the existing open attempt and did not create another QR code. It directed the user to finish or check the open QR Ph payment first.
Pass
PM-09
Complete a PayMongo test-mode payment for a pending QR Ph transaction.
PayMongo confirms the payment, the repayment is recorded once, the loan balance is updated, and the receipt is available.
After the test payment was confirmed, the QR status changed to paid. One repayment was recorded, the loan balance was reduced, and the receipt was available. The balance had remained unchanged until that confirmation.
Pass
PM-10
Deliver a PayMongo webhook with a valid signature and a current timestamp for a confirmed payment on a pending QR Ph transaction.
The signature is accepted and the repayment is recorded once.
The signed webhook was accepted. The matching pending transaction was marked paid, one repayment was stored, and the receipt was linked to that PayMongo transaction.
Pass
PM-11
Deliver a PayMongo webhook with a missing signature, an incorrect signature, or a timestamp outside the allowed window.
The webhook is rejected and no repayment is recorded.
The webhook was rejected. The loan balance, the payment list, and the pending QR Ph transaction were not updated from that request.
Pass
PM-12
Try to delete a repayment that is linked to a verified PayMongo QR Ph transaction.
The linked payment, receipt, and PayMongo record stay on file.
The deletion was refused. The message stated that the payment is linked to a verified PayMongo QR Ph transaction and was not deleted.
Pass

PT-01
Open the administrator dashboard, collector dashboard, payments, reports, and borrower account pages without signing in.

PT-02
Sign in with a wrong password or with an inactive account.
The protected pages do not open.
Login failed. An error message was shown, and the administrator, collector, and borrower pages were not accessible without a valid session.
Pass
PT-03
Sign in as a borrower and open administrator pages for users, reports, loan release, payments, and expenses.
The borrower cannot open administrator pages.
The borrower was returned to the borrower dashboard. Administrator records and actions were not displayed.
Pass
PT-04
Sign in as a collector and open administrator-only pages for user management, reports, borrower approval, and loan release.
The collector cannot open administrator-only pages.
The collector was returned to the collector dashboard or received an unauthorized response. Administrator-only records were not changed.
Pass
PT-05
Submit a form that creates or changes a record, such as registration, payment, expense, user, or loan release, without a valid security token.
The change is rejected.
The submission was rejected. No registration, payment, expense, user, or loan release was saved from that request.
Pass
PT-06
Enter database query text in the sign-in, search, and record fields.
The text is treated as ordinary input. It does not change which records are returned or saved.
The entered text was handled as data. Sign-in failed, or the search returned only ordinary matches. No extra accounts, loans, or payments were exposed or changed.
Pass
PT-07
Enter script markup in names, remarks, search, and other text fields, then open the page that displays those fields.
The markup is shown as text and does not run in the browser.
The stored text was displayed as plain text. The markup was not executed, and the surrounding page continued to load normally.
Pass
PT-08
Sign in as a borrower and open another borrower's loan, payment history, receipt, or uploaded document by using that record's address.
The other borrower's records do not open.
The request was refused, or the page showed only the signed-in borrower's own records. The other receipt and document were not downloaded.
Pass
PT-09
Sign in as a collector and open a client, loan, or payment that is not assigned to that collector.
The unassigned record does not open for collection.
Access was denied and the collector was returned to the collector dashboard. No payment was recorded for the unassigned loan.
Pass
PT-10
Upload a loan document that is not a JPG, PNG, or PDF file, and upload a file larger than 8 MB.
The file is rejected and is not stored with the application.
The upload was rejected. The message identified the allowed file types or the 8 MB limit, and the file was not saved with the loan documents.
Pass
PT-11
Sign out, then use the browser Back button or the previous session to open a protected page.
The ended session cannot open protected pages.
Sign-out cleared the session. The protected page redirected to the sign-in page, and the previous account data was no longer shown.
Pass
PT-12
Open a password-reset link that is missing, expired, or already used, and submit a new password.
The password is not changed.
The page showed that the token was invalid or expired and asked for a new reset link. The existing password still signed in, and the rejected link did not set a new password.
