In addition to payments recorded at the lending office, FRAMS includes PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph. A collector starts the code for an assigned loan when the method is bank transfer or e-wallet. The borrower pays by scanning that code. The QR image is not a payment confirmation. While the QR payment page remains open, FRAMS polls the PayMongo Payment Intent API and records the repayment only after the provider status is succeeded and the paid amount matches the pending transaction. A webhook handler is present in the application and may also post a verified event, but it is optional for this collector workflow. Showing the code, returning to the browser, or waiting for a local timer does not, by itself, record the repayment. Cash is recorded directly by authorized staff. Bank transfer and e-wallet may also be recorded directly, without a QR code, by an administrator or the assigned collector.

[69]
The system includes PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph for supported non-cash collection transactions. According to the implemented workflow, an authorized collector initiates a QR payment for an assigned loan. The corresponding repayment is recorded after the Payment Intent status is confirmed as succeeded and the paid amount has been matched to the pending transaction. While the QR page is open, FRAMS polls the Payment Intent API for that confirmation. A webhook may also post a verified event when configured, but the collector workflow does not require a delivered webhook in order to record a matching paid intent. Cash payments and other authorized payment records may be entered directly by authorized personnel. Successful QR Ph collection depends on valid PayMongo API credentials and network access to retrieve Payment Intent status.

PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph. A collector-initiated QR payment for bank transfer or e-wallet. The repayment is recorded after PayMongo reports a succeeded Payment Intent for the matching amount, through status polling on the open QR page or through an optional webhook. Displaying the QR image is not confirmation of payment.

FRAMS is required to perform the following functions. Loan restructuring, a separate risk-analyst role, and machine-learning scores are outside this system and are not listed.

[232]
Payments and penalties. The system records a payment with amount, date, method, collector, optional reference, and a penalty portion. It rejects a payment that exceeds the remaining balance plus the penalty still due. It recomputes the balance and the installment status. For an unpaid installment past its due date, it computes a penalty of 1 percent, 2 percent, 3 percent, or 5 percent of the installment amount for daily, weekly, semi-monthly, and monthly loans. Cash, bank transfer, and e-wallet can be recorded at once by an administrator or the assigned collector. For bank transfer or e-wallet, a collector may instead start PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph. That repayment is stored only after the Payment Intent is confirmed as succeeded, either through status polling on the open QR page or through an optional verified webhook.

Figure 5.7 shows how an administrator or the assigned collector records a cash, bank transfer, or e-wallet payment on a released loan. A staff member selects the loan, and the page shows the remaining balance and the penalty due. Penalty due is computed from unpaid installments whose due date has passed, at 1 percent for a daily schedule, 2 percent for weekly, 3 percent for semi-monthly, and 5 percent for monthly, after penalty amounts already recorded on payments are applied. The receipt can then be viewed, printed, or downloaded.

[505]
FRAMS was implemented as a browser-based application for a local Apache, PHP, and MySQL environment. The documented local setup is XAMPP, with the application placed under the web server document root. Authorized users open the pages in a web browser. The server renders each page, checks the signed-in role, reads and writes the database, and returns HTML. There is no separate mobile application and no separate public web service for the screens. The payments page uses two authenticated JSON lookups. One loads the selected loan and its remaining balance into the payment form. The other refreshes a pending PayMongo QR Ph transaction by retrieving its Payment Intent status and returns the stored result. A QR Ph repayment is posted only after that status is succeeded and the amount matches, or after an optional verified webhook posts the same confirmation.

[644]
Payment and collection management. The payments page lists collections and records a payment for an authorized loan. The form shows the borrower, the amount already paid, and the remaining balance. The fields stored are the amount, date, collector, method, optional reference number, and penalty portion. The receipt number uses the prefix RJRR, the date, and a numeric sequence. The borrower can view that borrower's payment history and download a receipt for that borrower's own payment. The collector works from assigned accounts. When the method is bank transfer or e-wallet, that collector can start PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph. The repayment, receipt, and balance change are saved only after Payment Intent polling on the open QR page, or an optional verified webhook, confirms success and the paid amount matches the pending transaction. An unverified, expired, or mismatched QR code does not post a payment, and a verified QR Ph payment is not deleted from the list. A payment that fails the amount checked in Section 4.1.1 is rejected.

The analysis of findings relates the implemented features and documented testing results of FRAMS to the objectives stated in Chapter I. It examines borrower and loan information management, the financial eligibility estimate, payment monitoring, notifications, and report generation. The eligibility assessment is an estimate based on the borrower’s financial information and configured lending criteria; it is not a prediction of loan default and does not replace the administrator’s approval decision. The findings are calculated from the twenty item-level questionnaire answers recorded for each respondent in the supplied workbook. Local PayMongo simulations are distinguished from external provider deliveries.

[838]
Borrowers can register and, after administrator approval, submit loan applications with the required supporting documents. The eligibility estimate uses declared income, existing financial obligations, the requested loan amount, the loan term, and configured lending criteria, including the debt-to-income limit. The estimate serves as a decision-support tool and does not automatically approve or reject a loan. The system also supports loan release, installment schedule generation, payment recording, and repayment monitoring. PayMongo QR Ph repayments should be treated as confirmed transactions only when the required payment-verification process has been completed successfully.
