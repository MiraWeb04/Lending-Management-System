=== OBJECTIVES / SOP / CONCLUSIONS ===
[27] Background of Study
[36] Statement of the Problem
[40] Specifically, the study seeks to answer the following questions:
[42] How can FRAMS improve the management of borrower information and loan applications?
[44] How can FRAMS assist in assessing borrower eligibility using declared income, existing financial obligations, requested loan amount, loan term, and configured lending criteria without treating the assessment as an automatic loan approval?
[46] How can FRAMS facilitate the monitoring of loan repayments, outstanding balances, installment schedules, and overdue accounts?
[48] How can FRAMS support the delivery of notifications concerning loan application decisions, agreement acceptance, loan releases, and payment transactions?
[50] How can FRAMS generate financial reports that support the monitoring of lending transactions and management decision-making?
[53] Objectives of the Study
[55] Specifically, the study aims to:
[56] Develop a borrower management module that supports borrower registration, administrator approval, client record management, online loan applications, and supporting document submission and review.
[57] Implement a rule-based eligibility assessment that evaluates borrower information, declared income, existing financial obligations, requested loan amount, loan term, and configured lending criteria to assist the administrator in reviewing loan applications.
[58] Develop loan processing and repayment monitoring functions that support interest computation, installment schedule generation, loan agreement acceptance, collector assignment, loan release, payment recording, penalty computation, and monitoring of outstanding balances.
[59] Implement notifications for relevant loan application decisions, agreement updates, loan releases, and payment transactions, consistent with the notification functions available in the developed system.
[60] Develop financial reporting functions that support the monitoring of loan releases, collections, outstanding balances, overdue accounts, and other relevant lending transactions.
[61] Evaluate the developed system in terms of functionality, usability, reliability, security, and performance using the evaluation instrument and verified responses of the intended users.
[63] Scope and Delimitations
[724] In relation to the problem stated in Chapter I, FRAMS centralizes borrower registration, loan requests, eligibility estimates, agreements, released loans, installment schedules, and payment records in one database. [842] Conclusions
[843] In relation to the first specific objective, FRAMS provides a borrower management module that supports registration, administrator approval, client records, online loan applications, and supporting document submission and review.
[844] In relation to the second specific objective, FRAMS computes a rule-based eligibility estimate from declared income, existing obligations, requested amount, loan term, and configured limits. The estimate is a decision-support result and does not automatically approve or reject a loan.
[845] In relation to the third specific objective, FRAMS supports interest computation, installment schedules, loan agreement acceptance, collector assignment, loan release, payment recording, penalty computation, and repayment monitoring. PayMongo QR Ph repayments are recorded after Payment Intent confirmation through status polling on the open QR page 
[846] In relation to the fourth specific objective, FRAMS sends in-application notifications for relevant application decisions, agreement updates, loan releases, and payment transactions, consistent with the notification functions available in the developed system.
[847] In relation to the fifth specific objective, FRAMS generates financial reports that support monitoring of loan releases, collections, outstanding balances, overdue accounts, and related lending transactions.
