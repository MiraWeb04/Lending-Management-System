
===== P36 =====

In addition to payments recorded at the lending office, FRAMS includes PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph. A collector starts the code for an assigned loan when the method is bank transfer or e-wallet. The borrower pays by scanning that code. The balance, receipt, and installment status change only after PayMongo sends a verified webhook and the paid amount matches the loan. Showing the code, returning to the browser, or waiting for a local timer does not record the repayment. Cash is recorded directly by authorized staff. Bank transfer and e-wallet may also be recorded directly, without a QR code, by an administrator or the assigned collector.

===== P67 =====

FRAMS includes PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph for bank transfer and e-wallet collection. Only a collector can start a QR code, and only for an assigned loan. A repayment is posted only after a verified PayMongo webhook. The integration depends on the configured secret key, webhook secret, and mode, which is test or live. Borrowers do not have a separate checkout page. The study does not include credit bureau integration, SMS integration, loan restructuring, deposits, savings, or investment accounts. Dependents and collateral notes may be stored, but they are not variables in the eligibility formula. The eligibility estimate remains rule-based and does not predict default. The study also does not establish that all recording errors have been eliminated or that the system has been deployed across multiple branches.

===== P217 =====

Payments and penalties. The system records a payment with amount, date, method, collector, optional reference, and a penalty portion. It rejects a payment that exceeds the remaining balance plus the penalty still due. It recomputes the balance and the installment status. For an unpaid installment past its due date, it computes a penalty of 1 percent, 2 percent, 3 percent, or 5 percent of the installment amount for daily, weekly, semi-monthly, and monthly loans. Cash, bank transfer, and e-wallet can be recorded at once by an administrator or the assigned collector. For bank transfer or e-wallet, a collector may instead start PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph. That repayment is stored only after a verified webhook.

===== P515 =====

FRAMS was implemented as a browser-based application for a local Apache, PHP, and MySQL environment. The documented local setup is XAMPP, with the application placed under the web server document root. Authorized users open the pages in a web browser. The server renders each page, checks the signed-in role, reads and writes the database, and returns HTML. There is no separate mobile application and no separate public web service for the screens. The payments page uses two authenticated JSON lookups. One loads the selected loan and its remaining balance into the payment form. The other returns the status of a PayMongo QR Ph transaction. A QR Ph repayment is posted only after the webhook is verified.

===== P666 =====

Payment and collection management. The payments page lists collections and records a payment for an authorized loan. The form shows the borrower, the amount already paid, and the remaining balance. The fields stored are the amount, date, collector, method, optional reference number, and penalty portion. The receipt number uses the prefix RJRR, the date, and a numeric sequence. The borrower can view that borrower's payment history and download a receipt for that borrower's own payment. The collector works from assigned accounts. When the method is bank transfer or e-wallet, that collector can start PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph. The repayment, receipt, and balance change are saved only after the webhook is verified. An unverified, expired, or mismatched QR code does not post a payment, and a verified QR Ph payment is not deleted from the list. A payment that fails the amount checked in Section 4.1.1 is rejected.
