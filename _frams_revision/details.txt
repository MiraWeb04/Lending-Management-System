[846] Recommendations 
[847] Based on the system’s current scope and the limitations identified in the study, the researchers recommend the following:
[848] SMS notifications. They may be added later if the lending office needs them and can support the cost. 
[849] Establish a database backup and recovery procedure. Schedule regular MySQL backups of the actual production database and document how authorized personnel can restore a backup. Confirm the deployed database name before using it in any instructions.
[850] Centralize business rules in configuration. Keep the debt-to-income limit, minimum and maximum loan amounts, interest rate, and penalty rates in controlled configuration or administrator settings so approved policy changes do not require direct edits to PHP files.
[851] Develop an administrator settings page that allows authorized personnel to update loan limits and penalty rates, with validation and an audit trail.
[852] Improve audit visibility by recording important actions—such as account approvals, loan releases, payment edits, and role changes—in a dedicated activity log for accountability and troubleshooting.

[1220] WM = [f(5) × 5 + f(4) × 4 + f(3) × 3 + f(2) × 2 + f(1) × 1] / N
[1221] where f represents the frequency of a response rating and N represents the total number of valid individual ratings.
[1222] 
[1223] Note. The overall totals aggregate the response ratings across all 20 statements. Each criterion-level mean is based on 128 individual ratings, while the overall mean is based on 640 individual ratings.
[1224] The overall weighted score was calculated as follows:
[1225] (341 × 5) + (297 × 4) + (2 × 3) = 1,705 + 1,188 + 6 = 2,899.
[1226] The total number of individual ratings was 32 respondents × 20 statements = 640.
[1227] Therefore, the overall weighted mean was 2,899 ÷ 640 = 4.5297, rounded to 4.53 and interpreted as Strongly Agree.
