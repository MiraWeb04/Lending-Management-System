[851] Recommendations
[852] Based on the system’s current scope and the limitations identified in the study, the researchers recommend the following, in order of priority.
[853] 1. Establish a database backup and recovery procedure. Schedule regular MySQL backups of the production database and document how authorized personnel can restore a backup. Confirm the deployed database name before using it in any restore instructions.
[854] 2. Improve auditability by recording important actions—such as account approvals, loan releases, payment edits, and role changes—in a dedicated activity log.

[856] 4. Keep the debt-to-income limit, minimum and maximum loan amounts, interest rate, and penalty rates in controlled configuration so approved policy changes do not require undocumented edits to application files.
[857] 5. Develop an administrator settings page, in a later enhancement, that allows authorized personnel to update loan limits and penalty rates, with validation and an audit trail. This page is not part of the current implementation.
[858] 6. SMS notifications may be added later if the lending office needs them and can support the cost. They are not implemented in the current system.
