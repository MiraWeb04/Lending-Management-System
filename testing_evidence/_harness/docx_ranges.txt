840|Login interface. The borrower login and the staff login are separate pages. Each verifies the account, creates the session, and opens the matching dashboard. A borrower who is not yet approved does not enter the borrower pages. A forgotten-password page can send a time-limited reset when mail is configured.
841|Public portal and registration. The public page introduces RJ and RR Finance Services, and the loan products shown there: salary, business, emergency, and vehicle financing. Registration stores a pending account. A success page tells the applicant to wait for review. The contact form stores an inquiry that an administrator can answer.
842|Dashboard monitoring. After login, the administrator sees the executive dashboard: total loans, active loans, amount released, amount collected, outstanding balance, and overdue loans, with charts of collections, releases, loan status, and expenses. The collector dashboard is limited to assigned clients, loans, schedules, and pending collections. The borrower dashboard summarizes that borrower's own application or loan.
843|User, collector, and profile management. Administrators add and edit users, assign the administrator, collector, or borrower role, and set an account active or inactive. The collectors page creates, edits, activates, and deactivates collector accounts and can search by name, username, email, or employee identifier. The profile page lets the signed-in user review the account and change the password. Passwords entered on these pages are hashed before they are stored.
844|Borrower approval and client management. The approval page lists registrations and records approval or a rejection. Approval is what allows the borrower to login, and an approval email can be sent when mail is configured. The client list supports search. Client details show the borrower, loan history, and the assigned collector. The administrator search box in the top bar opens the client list.
845|Loan application management. An approved borrower who is allowed to apply completes five steps: personal information, employment and income, loan details, requirements, and review. Identity fields taken from registration stay locked. Address and mobile number can be updated. Required files are a valid government ID, proof of income, proof of billing, a selfie holding a valid ID, and an additional supporting document. Submission stores the application as pending review, stores the files, and saves the eligibility estimate. The administrator verifies or rejects each file and can approve the application, reject it, or ask for more documents. Remarks are stored. The borrower can open a loan-status page when a trackable application exists. Submission does not release funds.
846|Financial risk assessment display. The estimate appears on the loan details step and again on the administrator's review of the saved application. The panel shows the status, income, existing debt, both debt-to-income figures, the requested amount, the estimated eligible amount, the maximum eligible amount, the proposed payment, the term, the interest rate, the reasons, and any documents still requested. The status labels are Eligible, Conditionally Eligible, and Not Eligible. The same disclaimer shown in the calculation is printed on the panel.
847|Agreement, release, and loan monitoring. The administrator generates the agreement. The borrower accepts it by entering the borrower’s name or declines it. Acceptance stores the name and the date and time. Release requires an active collector. It creates a loan number in the form RJRR-LOAN, the year, and a six-digit sequence, together with the release record, the loan, and the installment rows. A release invoice can be opened. The loan list and loan details show the amount, term, frequency, status, balance, schedule, and payments. Status values used for loans include Active, Overdue, and Paid. The borrower sees only the released loan on My Loan.
848|Payment and collection management. The payments page lists collections and records a payment for an authorized loan. The form shows the borrower, the amount already paid, and the remaining balance. The fields stored are the amount, date, collector, method, optional reference number, and penalty portion. The receipt number uses the prefix RJRR, the date, and a numeric sequence. The borrower can view that borrower's payment history and download a receipt for that borrower's own payment. The collector works from assigned accounts. When the method is bank transfer or e-wallet, that collector can start PayMongo Dynamic QR Ph. The repayment, receipt, and balance change are saved only after the webhook is verified. An unverified, expired, or mismatched QR code does not post a payment, and a verified QR Ph payment is not deleted from the list. A payment that fails the amount checked in Section 4.1.1 is rejected.
849|Penalties, expenses, and reports. The penalty page shows the outstanding penalty and the penalty amounts collected in the selected period. A collected penalty appears when the penalty portion was entered with the payment. Expenses store description, amount, date, and category. Reports are limited to the administrator and contain six views: dashboard analytics, loan reports, payment reports, borrower reports, collector performance, and financial summary. The period may be daily, monthly, yearly, or a custom range. The current view can be exported as a PDF.
850|Notifications. The notifications page lists messages for the signed-in user and can mark them read. The top bar shows the unread count. Events such as approval, an application decision, agreement action, and loan release create an in-application notice. Matching email functions exist for account approval and rejection, application submission and decision, agreement decision, release, payment received, and a fully paid loan. The application notice does not depend on mail delivery.
852|Testing Strategy
853|Testing is documented through unit, integration, system, PayMongo integration, and security test cases, with user acceptance testing reported separately. The final status of each test must be based on an actual execution record and supporting evidence. A test should be marked Passed only when its observed result matches the expected result. Failed, blocked, and not-yet-tested cases must remain separately identified until they are resolved or accurately reported as limitations.
856|Unit Testing
857|Unit testing examines whether a single function or page accepts valid input, rejects input that breaks its own rule, and shows or stores the intended result. The cases cover login, registration, the eligibility gate, document upload, and payment amount checks, because those rules are implemented as discrete checks.
